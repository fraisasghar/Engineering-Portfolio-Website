01 · ABOUT

About Me

FA · MECH.ENG
Who I Am

I'm a mechanical engineering student at NUST who turns ideas into CAD models, and CAD models into things that actually move. SolidWorks and ANSYS are where a concept gets stress tested long before it ever touches a machine tool.

I build that same instinct into software. COMSOL and Proteus let me simulate before I build anything physical, while Python and MATLAB turn raw numbers into decisions I can actually trust.

Outside class, I'm in the workshop or behind a keyboard, building the small tools that make engineering work faster. This portfolio is one of them.

I'm also a research student working on heat exchangers, exploring an innovative approach to increase the rate of heat transfer beyond what standard designs achieve.
